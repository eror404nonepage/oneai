Install the App

OneAI runs as a native-style app on your phone. No store, no download.

iPhone & iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari. Chrome on iOS cannot install apps.
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm with Add. The icon appears on your home screen.
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. OneAI installs like any other app.
Desktop
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Confirm Install.

Installing adds a shortcut and enables full-screen mode. It requests no permissions and stores nothing beyond your session.
